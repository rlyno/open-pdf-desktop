<h1 align="center">Open PDF Desktop</h1>

<p align="center">
  A free PDF editor and toolbox for your computer. Edit, merge, split, compress, sign and
  protect PDFs without uploading them anywhere.
</p>

<p align="center">

**Everything happens on your computer.** No account, no server, no ads. Your files never leave
your machine.

## Download

Downloads appear on the [Releases page](https://github.com/rlyno/open-pdf-desktop/releases) for
macOS, Windows and Linux. The first release is coming soon.

## What you can do

**Editor.** Open a PDF (or images, which become pages) and it goes straight to the editor:

- Draw, highlight, type text, add boxes, white-out, signatures and images on the page.
- Reorder pages by dragging thumbnails. Rotate, duplicate, crop, delete or extract them.
- Insert pages from another PDF, or blank pages.
- Undo and redo everything. Saving makes a new copy; your original file is never changed.

**Tools**, right inside the editor:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Organize** | Merge, split, reorder, rotate, remove pages, 2 or 4 pages per sheet, crop |
| **Convert** | Images to PDF, PDF to images, compress |
| **Edit** | Watermark, page numbers, signature or image, document info, fill in and flatten forms |
| **Security** | Add or remove a password (AES-256) |

You can also run one tool on many files at once, for example compress 20 PDFs in one go.

The app speaks English or Indonesian, depending on your system language.

## Good to know

- Text you add, watermarks and page numbers support Latin letters only.
- Text that is already in a PDF can't be edited. White-out covers it on the page, but the text
  underneath stays in the file.
- Light, Medium and Strong compression turn pages into pictures, so the text can no longer be selected.
- No OCR or Word/Excel conversion yet.
